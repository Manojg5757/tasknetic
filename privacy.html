Privacy Policy

How Tasknetic collects, uses and protects your information when you visit this site or buy our guide.

Who we are

This website sells Take Back Your Time: The 30-Day Digital Detox Guide, a digital product by Tasknetic. For any privacy question, contact us at taskneticaiagents@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you buy: checkout and payment happen on SuperProfile and its payment partners. They collect the details needed to complete your order, such as your name, email address, phone number and payment information. We receive your order details (for example name, email and purchase record) so we can deliver the guide and support you. We never see or store your full card or bank details.

When you contact us: we keep the emails you send us so we can answer them.

When you browse this page: we use the Meta Pixel (a small piece of code from Meta Platforms) to measure visits and purchases from our ads. It may use cookies and collect information such as pages viewed, buttons clicked, browser and device type, and IP address.

How we use your information

  • To deliver your purchase and send download links.
  • To respond to support, delivery and refund requests.
  • To measure and improve our ads and this website.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and advertising

The Meta Pixel helps us understand which ads work. You can limit this by adjusting your browser cookie settings, using your Facebook/Instagram ad preferences, or blocking third-party cookies. The page still works if you do.

Third parties we rely on

SuperProfile (checkout and delivery), its payment gateways (UPI, cards and net banking), Meta Platforms (advertising measurement), Render (website hosting) and Google (email). Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

How long we keep data

We keep order and support records for as long as needed to provide the product, handle refunds, and meet legal and tax requirements, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop non-essential communication, by emailing taskneticaiagents@gmail.com. We will reply within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
